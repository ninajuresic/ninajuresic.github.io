HealthTalk

AI-Powered Clinical Documentation Platform

Enterprise AI platform transforming clinical conversations into compliant medical documentation.

Once Upon a Time…

Picture this: It's 6 PM. Dr. Sarah has just finished seeing her last patient, a heartfelt conversation about managing chronic pain. But her day isn't over. Not even close.

She opens her laptop and begins the dreaded task: writing clinical reports. One by one, she recalls each conversation, structures her notes into proper medical format, ensures everything meets compliance requirements. It's important work, but it's not why she became a doctor.

Hours pass. It's now 9 PM. Dr. Sarah closes her laptop, exhausted, not from healing patients, but from paperwork. And tomorrow? She'll do it all over again.

Enter HealthTalk

What if Dr. Sarah could just… talk to her patients and have everything documented automatically? What if the AI could listen, understand, and transform those conversations into perfectly structured clinical reports, while she focused on the person in front of her?

That's exactly what HealthTalk does. It's not magic, it's thoughtful AI-powered design that turns hours of administrative burden into minutes of review time.

Dr. Sarah now leaves the clinic at 6:15 PM.

Same patient care, zero overtime paperwork.

Core Design Methodology

AI as Design Partner

Designing an AI-powered product required using AI as a core tool in the design process itself. This section explores how I integrated AI tools throughout research, prototyping, and validation.

Core Principle: Real Users First, AI as Amplifier

While AI tools dramatically accelerated my process, all major design decisions were validated with real clinicians. AI helped me move faster, test more assumptions, and arrive at user interviews with sharper questions.

Three Ways AI Transformed My Design Process

AI Personas

Created realistic clinician personas to test ideas throughout the day

Figma Make

Built and tested functional prototypes in hours instead of days

Iterative Testing

Validated assumptions daily before committing to high-fidelity designs

AI

ChatGPT Personas: Always-On Design Partners

Why I Built AI Personas

Real user research is essential but happens in discrete sessions. Between those sessions, I needed a way to continuously pressure-test assumptions, explore edge cases, and refine flows without waiting for the next round of interviews.

Using ChatGPT, I created detailed personas based on real research, emergency room physicians, mental health therapists, general practitioners, each with specific workflows, pain points, technical comfort levels, and motivations.

How I Used Them Daily

  • Quick concept validation: Rapid feedback on new design directions
  • Edge case exploration: Surfacing scenarios before real user testing
  • Workflow walkthroughs: Talking through entire user journeys to identify friction points

Example: Template Flow

After designing the template creation flow, I spent an afternoon querying three AI personas about the step-by-step process. This surfaced 8 specific usability concerns, all of which I addressed before showing the design to real users.

"The AI personas never replaced real users, they made me better prepared to learn from them."

Key Takeaway

AI tools didn't replace user research, they made every stage between research sessions more productive. I could test more ideas, catch more problems, and arrive at user interviews with better questions and sharper hypotheses.

The Conversation Begins

Dr. Sarah sits down with her patient and taps "Start Recording" on her screen. From that moment, HealthTalk becomes her invisible assistant, listening attentively to every word. The interface shows her exactly who's speaking, captures the conversation in real-time, and displays her custom checklist right on screen.

Three Minutes Later…

The consultation wraps up. Dr. Sarah says goodbye to her patient and taps "Generate Report." She grabs a coffee. By the time she's back at her desk, literally three minutes later, a complete, structured clinical report is waiting for her. Formatted in proper SOAP structure. All key details from the conversation included. Ready to submit.

Need it in Spanish for the patient? One click. Ready to paste into the EHR? Copy and done. What used to take 45 minutes now takes 3 minutes of quick review and approval.

But Wait… Every Doctor is Different

Dr. Sarah is a pain management specialist. Her colleague Dr. James is a psychiatrist. Their documentation needs are completely different. Cookie-cutter templates? Not happening.

That's why HealthTalk lets doctors build their own custom templates, no coding required. A simple form-based builder with intuitive inputs and a live preview. The entire healthcare organization can standardize their documentation while each doctor personalizes their experience.

And It Goes Wherever You Go

Because doctors don't always work from their desks. They're making house calls, consulting in different wings, or reviewing notes between appointments. HealthTalk works beautifully on mobile too.

Design Principles I Applied

Trust Before Automation

AI supports decisions, never hides logic. Always show the reasoning and allow human override.

Cognitive Load Reduction

Clear hierarchy and progressive disclosure ensure information is presented at the right time.

Clinical Alignment

Workflows mirror real clinical thinking patterns, making the tool feel natural to medical professionals.

Scalability

Reusable components and patterns ensure consistency and faster iteration across features.

Human-in-the-Loop AI

Clinicians always remain in control. AI provides suggestions and automation, but final decisions rest with medical professionals.

Reflection

Here's what I learned: The best healthcare technology doesn't try to replace doctors, it gives them their time back. Every minute saved on paperwork is a minute they can spend with patients, with their families, or simply recovering from the emotional weight of the job.

My job wasn't to show off what AI could do. It was to create interfaces doctors could trust completely, where they could see the AI's reasoning, override decisions, and always feel in control. Because in healthcare, trust isn't a nice-to-have. It's the product.

Key Learnings

AI tools as design amplifiers

Using ChatGPT and Figma Make didn't replace user research, it made every moment between sessions more productive.

Trust is the foundation

In high-stakes domains like healthcare, transparency and human control aren't optional, they're the core of the product experience.

Rapid prototyping changes everything

Being able to build functional prototypes in hours fundamentally changed how I approach design, moving from static validation to continuous experimentation.

"Designing AI products means designing trust, transparency, and human agency into every interaction."

No layers of process, just close collaboration with the founders, moving fast from discovery to delivered interfaces. End-to-end ownership of the product design.

How I Work Here

Rather than building static mockups, I produce working coded prototypes using Zed and Claude. The engineering team gets something they can run, test, and build directly from, and founders get a real feel for the product before a line of production code is written.

But Kaphera is also where I’m discovering something bigger: a genuinely new way of working as a digital product designer.

Zed + Claude Coded prototypes Early-stage discovery End-to-end ownership

What I’m discovering

AI as an everyday tool changes what it means to be a product designer

For most of my career, the designer’s job was to get as close to the real thing as possible, wireframes, then mockups, then prototypes, and then hand them off. Each step was a translation layer, and something always got lost.

Working with Zed and Claude, those layers collapse. I design in the medium the product will actually live in. I don’t describe what something should look like, I build it, see it, adjust it, and ship it. The result is the design.

What AI gives me isn’t shortcuts, it’s execution speed on the parts that aren’t the thinking. The judgment about what’s right for the user, the decisions about hierarchy and flow, what to simplify and what to keep, those are still entirely mine.

But the gap between an idea and something you can actually put in front of a person and test, that gap is now measured in hours, not weeks. That changes everything about how early and how often you can get real feedback.

Domain

Platform

The Problem

Money is a system for directing economic activity. Right now it flows toward financial return, which is easy to measure, while damage to biodiversity, soil, water, and human wellbeing goes unaccounted for.

Capital keeps flowing toward things that pay off financially even when they cause that damage. The structural problem is measurement: what gets measured gets funded.

The Answer

ION changes where money flows. Think of it as a bank account, following the Nature Banking principle, where capital grows according to a natural growth curve while directly funding The Works: a venture developing R&D for products aligned with human wellbeing and nature.

Returns cycle back to three places: the ION holder, further product development, and the guardians of nature. An ION account exists alongside a regular account, serving a different purpose, connecting to a different economy.
